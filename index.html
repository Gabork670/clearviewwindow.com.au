What we do

Detailing, not just a wash

A rushed window clean leaves the glass streaked and the tracks full of grit. Every job here covers the parts most cleaners skip.

Residential window cleaning

Inside and out, glass, frames, sills and tracks. Ground and first floor covered from ladders and water fed poles.

Balustrades and glass fences

Frameless panels cleaned both sides, along with the spigots and handrails. Stairs, balconies and pool surrounds.

Solar panel cleaning

Dust and bird mess removed with pure water and a soft brush. No detergents and nothing abrasive near the panel surface.

Track and frame detailing

Sliding door tracks vacuumed, scrubbed and wiped dry. Doors run properly again instead of grinding on built up grit.

Flyscreen wash

Screens removed, washed, dried and refitted to the right opening. Insect mesh comes back to its original colour.

Small commercial

Shopfronts, offices and strata common areas on a set schedule. Weekly, fortnightly or monthly, invoiced monthly.

Privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026

ClearView Window Detailing (ABN 90 197 553 089) is a window cleaning business in Sydney. This page explains what happens to your details when you contact us or visit this website.

Details you send us

When you call, text or email, we receive what you choose to send. Usually that is your name, phone number, email address, suburb or address, and photos or videos of the job.

We use these details only to quote, book and carry out the work, to invoice you, and to follow up about the job. We do not sell them or pass them to anyone for marketing.

Invoices and payments are handled through Square, which processes payment details under its own privacy policy.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and to see which pages and buttons are used, for example how many people tap the Call button. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology to do this. It collects information such as the pages you view, roughly where you are, the type of device and browser you use, and how you arrived at the site.

We see this information only as totals and trends. It does not tell us who you are.

Google processes this data under its own policies. You can read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

To stop Google Analytics collecting data about your visits, you can block cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Fonts

The text on this website uses fonts loaded from Google Fonts. To deliver them, Google's servers receive your device's internet address.

Your details, your call

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to have them deleted once any job and invoice are finished. Email info@clearviewwindow.com.au and we will reply within a few business days.
